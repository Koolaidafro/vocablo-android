# Vocablo plan (summary)

The full, living plan is kept in the project's decision doc. This file records what's settled.

## Settled decisions

- **Platform:** native Android, Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3. Min Android 8.0 (API 26).
- **Storage:** everything on the phone (Room for cards, DataStore for settings). No card limit.
- **Region:** Latin American Spanish by default; Mexico, Caribbean, Andean, Rioplatense and Spain available. Region changes pronouns (vosotros, vos), vocabulary notes, the AI tutor and text-to-speech voice.
- **Spaced repetition:** FSRS. Swipe right = Good, swipe left = Again.
- **Verbs:** the 2,000 most common verbs in tiers of 100, 250, 500, 1,000 and 2,000, generated by a rules-plus-exceptions engine (about 150 KB for all 2,000).
- **Practice settings:** tenses unlock in stages (present; preterite and imperfect; future and conditional; then subjunctive). Nothing unchecked is ever quizzed.
- **Onboarding:** region, daily goal, then an optional eight-question placement check.
- **Adding cards:** built-in offline dictionary (Wiktionary + Tatoeba), free online fallback, optional AI. Duplicate check on every new card; conjugated forms point to their infinitive.
- **Engagement:** XP, streaks with earned freezes, daily quests, weekly chest, per-verb medals, five offline mini-games (Verb Rain, Tug of War, Sí o No, Parejas, Cuatro), local reminders (at most two a day).
- **Mascot:** Tuzo the gopher, with idle, correct, win, oops and sleepy animations.
- **Themes:** Jade (default), Océano, Atardecer, Lavanda, Grafito, each light and dark, plus System mode, wallpaper colors and pure black.
- **AI:** optional, bring your own key (Claude, OpenAI, Grok, any OpenAI-compatible endpoint), keys encrypted with Android Keystore and never backed up.

## Roadmap

1. **Foundation** (this phase): project setup, CI, themes, five-tab shell, verb engine core.
2. **Cards and swipe study:** decks and cards with dictionary lookup and duplicate check, FSRS swipe study, home screen, CSV/Anki import, backup.
3. **Verbs:** full 2,000-verb data, conjugation drill, practice settings, onboarding placement.
4. **Sentence builder and games:** sentence exercises, the five mini-games, XP, streaks, quests, reminders, Tuzo animations.
5. **AI tutor:** provider interface, secure key storage, scenario chat.
6. **Polish and release:** text-to-speech, widgets, foldables, accessibility, Play Store.
